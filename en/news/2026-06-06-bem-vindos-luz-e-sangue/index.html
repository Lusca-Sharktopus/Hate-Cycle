Welcome to Light & Blood!

New WorkLight & Blood

After promises upon promises and dates being pushed back further and further, it’s finally here! In partnership with TV Palavra Viva and Adolas Podcast, I bring you Light & Blood, an RPG campaign aimed at a Christian audience, but perfectly enjoyable by everyone!

The Christian community has historically been quite averse to the RPG medium. Fear campaigns in the 90s created a very particular view in the church’s collective consciousness of what is nothing more than an art form; a medium through which stories can be told. After all, if Christian books and movies are widely consumed and recognized as ways of glorifying the name of the Lord, I can’t see how a tabletop RPG can’t serve the same purpose!!
