Avialuna's Pentapods
by u/LuscaSharktopus in SpeculativeEvolution

Coelacanths were one of the five animal species brought from Earth to Avialuna around 110 million years ago, quickly spreading and developing into numerous lineages. Among those lineages are the pentapods, terrestrial vertebrates with five limbs, in the form of two wings and three legs.

The wings are pectoral fins, homologous to tetrapod forelimbs, while pentapod forelimbs are pelvic fins, homologous to tetrapod hindlimbs. The single hindlimb is an adapted anal fin, which is lost in tetrapods.

The main hypothesis for their evolution is that they first developed wings in order to hunt flying crabs (Avialuna's equivalent to insects), which would fly onto land, forcing the fish to learn how to get back to the water by crawling and, eventually, walking.
