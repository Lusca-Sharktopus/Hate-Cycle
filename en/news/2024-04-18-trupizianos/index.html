The Symbiont is part of a kingdom of multicellular organisms closely related to the Amoebozoa, called Symbiozoa.

Symbiozoans have no specialized cells, but the cells that form their bodies act as a neural system. They are conscious creatures.

As rodas crescem inicialmente como um a grande unha, que se solta do pé do Trupiziano e passa a se mover livremente. Trupizianos podem se mover a mais de 90km/h nos ambientes certos, mas podem usar os simbiozoários para se locomover em terrenos mais difíceis https://t.co/tk7kBnSDAm

The wheels initially grow as one big nail, which detaches from the Trupizian’s foot and starts moving freely.

Trupizians can move at over 90 km/h in the right environments, but can use symbiozoans to get around on harder terrain.
