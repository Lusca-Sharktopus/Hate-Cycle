About the project

The world of Hate Cycle is one of inherent magic and wonder, even if hidden underneath many layers of fear, oppression and hate. It’s a world of dragons, goblins, mages and dieties that hide from humanity out of fear of capture and containment. A world where all of your most wonderful dreams can come true, but you can’t talk about it, or else you will be silenced.

Something has left a deep scar that hasn’t fully healed yet. Something cut off the wings of fairies, something made aliens flee back to their planets. A terrible cataclysm rained down into the cracks and crevices of this world and stories are passed down mouth to mouth, parent to children, warning of the dangers of merely being.

But even then, every once in a while, something makes its way through; a woman in Wales meets a dragon; someone has a demonic experience; a man adopts a child with magical powers; little by little, this world is healing, and each and every story told here is a step into that direction.

Hate Cycle is the passion project of Lusca Sharktopus, whose real name is Lucas Pereira da Silva; initially idealized as a webcomic protagonized by the superheroes Apollo and The Amphibious, born out of an immensurable love for the superhero genre, Hate Cycle soon became more than that. Ideas of spin-offs games, comics and books, a Tabletop RPG system, as well as a change of plans turning Hate Cycle from a webcomic into an animated show, transformed the project into something bigger and greater. By this point, Hate Cycle was born. A shared universe, from the likeness of the MCU or Star Wars, united by the vision of a boy who grew into a man while dreaming about this world.

About the author

Affur Akmosh! I'm Lusca Sharktopus, author of the stories you’ll read, watch, listen to and play on this website! I've always loved drawing and creating stories since I was a kid; I dreamed of being the author of animated series and used to draw comic books. I enjoy creating worlds, languages, speculating about the evolution of organisms, and designing magic systems – Worldbuilding is easily my favorite part of the process – and in recent years I’ve been truly invested in it, creating in 2021 the Hate Cycle universe, a shared universe where I can tell (almost) all of my stories, united in a single world!

In addition, I’m a Biology major, a graphic designer, a devout christian as well as a musician at my church and I create all the covers and logos for the stories here myself. I was also Art Director at Safe Haven, a project focused on telling stories through Tabletop RPG!

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026.

Hate Cycle is the personal website of Lusca Sharktopus (Lucas Pereira da Silva). This page explains, in plain words, what data the website keeps, why, and how you can ask to see it or delete it. Questions and requests: lucas.blito@gmail.com.

Reading requires nothing

You can read, watch and browse the whole website without an account. The website has no ads, uses no analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) and does not sell or share data with anyone.

If you create an account

An account lets you comment and, once the feature is available, keep your bookmarks. With it, the website keeps:

  • your email address, so you can sign in and recover your password. It never appears on the website;
  • the name you choose to show on your comments;
  • if you sign in with Google, Discord or Twitter: your account identifier on that platform and the public information it shares (name and picture). Your password for those platforms never reaches the website;
  • your comments, which are public, with your name, on the page where you wrote them;
  • your bookmarks: where you stopped in each chapter.

This data is stored on Supabase, the service that hosts the website’s accounts and database.

What is kept in your browser

The website sets no cookies of its own. It keeps only these in your browser:

  • your session, so you stay signed in;
  • your preferences: language, reading order and the sorting of the Stories page;
  • bookmarks, if you use them without signing in (once you sign in, your account takes priority);
  • a mark so the opening animation plays only once per visit.

None of this is sent outside the website. You can delete it at any time by clearing this website’s data in your browser.

Content from other platforms

Some pages show content from other platforms: YouTube videos and posts from Twitter, Reddit and Instagram. When that content loads, those platforms may use their own cookies and receive data such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies. The same applies to Google, Discord and Twitter when you use one of them to sign in.

Comments on some stories use Disqus, which has its own accounts and privacy rules.

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log technical access data (such as your IP address) to keep the service running and secure.

Your rights

Under Brazil’s General Data Protection Law (LGPD), you may ask at any time to see the data the website has about you, to correct it or to delete it. To delete your account, write to lucas.blito@gmail.com from the account’s email address. When an account is deleted, its name, comments and bookmarks are deleted too.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page, with the update date at the top.
