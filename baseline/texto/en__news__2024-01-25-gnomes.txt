Gnomes are born from a relatively rare arcane phenomenon called "stellarization." Despite the means by which this process occurs remaining unknown, beings originating from it (referred to as stellars) are easily identifiable. Any non-sentient living being, such as mushrooms, plants, and corals, is susceptible to this phenomenon, which alters the morphology of the organism and grants it an almost sentient level of consciousness. Gnomes are one of the three known types of stellars, resulting from the stellarization of mushrooms. Different mushroom species appear to have unique characteristics attributed to their Gnome form.
