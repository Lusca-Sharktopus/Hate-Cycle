## About the author

<img class="autor-foto" src="/other images/1000172301.png" alt="Lusca Sharktopus">

Affur Akmosh! I'm *Lusca Sharktopus*, author of the stories you’ll read, watch, listen to and play on this website!

I've always loved drawing and creating stories since I was a kid; I dreamed of being the author of animated series and used to draw comic books. I enjoy creating worlds, languages, speculating about the evolution of organisms, and designing magic systems – Worldbuilding is easily my favorite part of the process – and in recent years I’ve been truly invested in it, creating in 2021 the *Hᴀᴛᴇ Cʏᴄʟᴇ* universe, a shared universe where I can tell (almost) all of my stories, united in a single world!

In addition, I’m a Biology major, a graphic designer, a devout christian as well as a musician at my church and I create all the covers and logos for the stories here myself. I was also Art Director at [Safe Haven](https://www.youtube.com/@safehavenoficial), a project focused on telling stories through Tabletop RPG!
