Photomorphs are one of the two sapient races of the planet Bulbix. It is not known exactly how Bulbix’s life came to be, but the life forms of this planet are living crystals.

Photomorphs are beings made almost purely of photons, with a single crystal as their solid core.

{{embed:x-fotomorfos-2}}

The other sapient race on Bulbix are the Geomorphs.

Their bodies are formed by multiple crystals called “cells”, held together by arcanoelectricity.
