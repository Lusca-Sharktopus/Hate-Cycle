## The Articulates

Proto-Articulate was a language spoken by the Articulates approximately 4 thousand years ago, when they began to develop sapience

The Articulate people are a race of sapient crabs. They did not evolve naturally, but were created by another sapient race, called the [[news/2023-08-19-conheca-os-talassimbiontes|Thalassymbiotes]].

Initially used as slaves and gladiators, the Thalassymbiotes decided to grant the Articulates greater intelligence to improve their ability to follow orders; they did not anticipate, however, that the Articulates becoming intelligent enough to rebel against them

## Phonology

Crabs have no vocalization system; the “phonology” of their language consists of syncopated rhythms produced with their claws. In a way, it recalls Morse code, but with far greater levels of complexity.

Articulate crabs have four claws: two larger and heavier ones, shaped like a sword and a shield, and two smaller ones, which they use to manipulate objects. The sword-claw is heavier at its movable segment, making it impractical to move consistently, while the shield-claw has a relatively small movable segment, but is still slower than the two manual claws. In “speech”, the shield-claw serves as a constant pulse that sets the base rhythm of the phrase.
