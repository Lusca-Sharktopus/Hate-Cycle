Solonians are a nomadic people that float through Solonia’s dense atmosphere, following the migratory patterns of Mauloys (creatures resembling giant Portuguese man o’ war).

They can become intangible and invisible at will.

{{embed:x-solonianos-2}}

Monocarians are unicellular organisms that live in a symbiotic relationship with Hygranocian technology, after centuries of the Hygranocians using the moon they live on, Pontus, as a giant dump.
