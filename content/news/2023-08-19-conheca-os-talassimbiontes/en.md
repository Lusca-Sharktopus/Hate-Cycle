Dolphins and octopuses are both extremely intelligent animals, capable of logical reasoning comparable to that of primates.

Octopuses, on one hand, are highly antisocial and fragile, which prevents them from developing any complex form of language. Dolphins, on the other hand, despite being extremely social and known for their ability to cooperate and communicate, have no means of manipulating the environment around them and building tools.

So, what happens when a parasitic octopus species develops a symbiotic relationship with dolphins, fusing its nervous system with that of the cetacean?

You get a Thalassymbiote: highly intelligent beings, endowed with the most important traits of both octopuses and dolphins. Beings capable of wielding tools and building civilizations.

The true citizens of the seas of Hᴀᴛᴇ Cʏᴄʟᴇ.
