The giant cave dragonflies, or "Kartekta'i" as they are called in the local language, are Meganeurids, a group believed to have been extinct since the Carboniferous, more than 300 million years ago.

Their exoskeleton has an iridescent blue-green color, possibly as a result of structural coloration. Their eyes are reddish and bioluminescent, which helps them see in the dark environment of the caves where they live.

These animals are almost 40 cm long with a 70 cm wingspan, which still intrigues our team as to how they manage to maintain their size, given that Carboniferous Meganeurids only achieved such a feat thanks to the oxygen levels of that era.
