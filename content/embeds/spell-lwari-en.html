Lwari’s BlessingDC 18
Circle V Enchantment
Type: Water (Myctis)
Cost: 24 Mana
Range: 2 Hex 🪄
Discovery: Year
Healing: 1d30 🪄

Attunement Castings: 8

Components: An Aquamarine Runic Pencil, a Flower of the species Caelluria mystica. (M)

After drawing the First Rune of the Moon in the air, the mage must dispel it as if using a Magical Attack, but instead of releasing the strike, they must place a flower of the species Caelluria mystica atop the energy. The Flower will float above the energy and, when the strike is released, a jet of water will heal the target for 1d30 Vitality.

The Player must also roll 1d100 and, on a result of 35 or lower – 40 if the ritual is performed at night and 45 under a New Moon –, the target is afflicted with the Regeneration Condition.
