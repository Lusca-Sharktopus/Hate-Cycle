Cytharr’s CurseDC 18
Circle V Evocation
Type: Fire (Anubis)
Cost: 24 Mana
Range: 2 Hex 🪄
Discovery: 1852
Damage: 1d30 Brn 🪄

Habituation Castings: 8

Components: A Jade Runic Pencil, a Flower of the species Caelluria mystica (M)

After drawing the First Rune of Death in the air, the mage must dispel it as if using a Magical Attack, but instead of releasing the strike, they must place a flower of the species Caelluria mystica atop the energy. The Flower will float atop the energy and, when the strike is released, a hand of fire will deal 1d30 Burning Damage to the target.

The Player must also roll 1d100 and, on a result of 35 or lower, the target is afflicted with the Burn Condition.

Lwari’s BlessingDC 18
Circle V Enchantment
Type: Water (Myctis)
Cost: 24 Mana
Range: 2 Hex 🪄
Discovery: 1854
Healing: 1d30 🪄

Habituation Castings: 8

Components: An Aquamarine Runic Pencil, a Flower of the species Caelluria mystica. (M)

After drawing the First Rune of the Moon in the air, the mage must dispel it as if using a Magical Attack, but instead of releasing the strike, they must place a flower of the species Caelluria mystica atop the energy. The Flower will float above the energy and, when the strike is released, a jet of water will heal the target for 1d30 Vitality.

The Player must also roll 1d100 and, on a result of 35 or lower – 40 if the ritual is performed at night and 45 under a New Moon –, the target is afflicted with the Regeneration Condition.

Ramosh’s DungeonDC 18
Circle V Transmutation
Type: Earth (Laukis)
Cost: 24 Mana
Range: 2 Hex 🪄
Discovery: 1873
Damage: 1d30 Bdg 🪄
Duration: 5 Turns

Habituation Castings: 9

Components: A Jade Runic Pencil, a Flower of the species Caelluria mystica (M)

After drawing the First Rune of the World in the air, the mage must dispel it as if using a Magical Attack, but instead of releasing the strike, they must place a flower of the species Caelluria mystica atop the energy. The Flower will float atop the energy and, when the strike is released, a sphere of green energy will fly to the target, followed by a wave of stones along the ground that will deal 1d30 Bludgeoning Damage to the target.

Vufir’s BladeDC 18
Circle V Evocation
Type: Air (Faris)
Cost: 24 Mana
Range: 2 Hex 🪄
Discovery: 1878
Damage: 1d30 Slh 🪄
Duration: 5 Turns

Habituation Castings: 8

Components: A Celestine Runic Pencil, a Flower of the species Caelluria mystica (M)

After drawing the First Rune of the Hermit in the air, the mage must dispel it as if using a Magical Attack, but instead of releasing the strike, they must place a flower of the species Caelluria mystica atop the energy.
