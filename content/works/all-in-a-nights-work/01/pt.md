<div class="s1 indent">It’s 04:00 AM. The sound of raindrops falling on top of the train’s roof would be a winsome invitation for an unconcerned night of sleep in the comfort of the seats encased in crimson velvet.</div>

<div class="s1">For most people in that voyage, it was; but not for two gentlemen sitting across from each other. Both are awake, but one of them doesn’t seem to be fully. His eyes soar through the increasingly blurry pages of a book as their weight becomes too much to bear.</div>

<div class="s1-gif"><img src="@/other images/nao-eu-nao-posso.gif" alt="NÃO… …EU NÃO POSSO…"></div>

<div class="s1 rr">Those are the thoughts running through the mind of a man who knows better than to sleep</div>

<div class="s1 mid gap">He closes his eyes<span class="ps"> for a fraction of a moment, shutting his book abruptly while opening them again and clenching his hand on his chest…</span></div>

<div class="s1 ps">The other man, with a newspaper in hands, notices and looks over his reading glasses.</div>

<div class="s1 ps">“Are you ok, sir?” He asks</div>

<div class="s1 ps">“Yes, I…” The tall man looks around, seemingly searching for something. He looks at yet another man, with a receding hairline and a voluminous moustache, peacefully sleeping beside him and lets out a sigh of relief. “...I’m sorry. I must have disturbed your reading.”</div>

<div class="s1 ps">“Oh, don’t worry”, the newspaper man responds</div>

<div class="s1 ps">A few minutes of awkward silence go by. The newspaper reader decides to break the ice.</div>

<div class="s1 ps">“You’re Italian, right?” he asks “Did you know the war with Ethiopia just ended?”</div>

<div class="s1 ps">“Yes, I did and yes, I am”, He responds quite awkwardly, “Is my accent that strong?”. He figures that, though small talk isn’t one of his strengths, it could be good to partake in conversation so as to not fall asleep.</div>

<div class="s1 ps">“It’s not, but I have a really good ear for these kinds of things”</div>

<div class="s1 ps">“Oh, I see…”</div>

<div class="s1 ps">“I’m sorry if I have disturbed <i>your</i> reading”. The man with the newspaper seems concerned about being rude. He glances at the book the tall man is holding. “La Sacra Bibbia” written in golden letters engraved on a black leather cover.</div>

<div class="s1 ps">“I wasn’t really reading”, the Italian man clarified. “I was looking for a specific text, but couldn’t remember where it was”. He then looked at the man sitting beside him, who’s peacefully sleeping with his head tilted backwards. The sleeping – and snoring – moustache man seems to be quite a few years older. “My friend here would know exactly where it is, but I don’t want to wake him up”.</div>

<div class="s1 ps">“I wish I could help”, the newspaper man added. “I’m not really a religious person. Oh, how rude of me! I didn’t ask for your name”</div>

<img class="jusep" src="/other images/Giuseppe.png" alt="Giuseppe">

<div class="s1 ps">“It’s Giuseppe, Giuseppe di Vario”</div>

<div class="s1 ps">“Mine’s Elijah Smith”</div>

<div class="s1 ps">As they shook hands, Giuseppe felt the weight of his eyes. He had one of those sleepy blinks where your eyes stay closed for a bit longer than what would be considered <i>“the blink of an eye”</i>. Once he opened them back, his vision gravitated towards the window Elijah’s back faced. It was like everything repeated; the same trees, the same cliffs, even the same animals doing the same motions. It was like the window was a TV screen repeating the same recording. It was not the first time Giuseppe had noticed it. It was like the train was going in endless circles, and Giuseppe was starting to get suspicious about the ouroboros-like pattern reality seemed to suddenly acquire.</div>

<div class="s1 ps">“I’m sorry if I seem distant or inattentive. I haven’t slept in a few days”, Giuseppe clarified.</div>

<div class="s1 ps">“Oh, that’s fine!” the man responded. “I’ve pulled my consecutive all-nighters as well! You see, I work on finances, and there’s this gentleman with whom I’ve been working for a while as a private financial advisor. He just bought a new property in Bristol and let me tell you, I’ve never been so fueled by coffee as I did in these past weeks in my whole bloody life!”</div>

<div class="s1 ps">“How interesting”, responded Giuseppe back, looking back again to the man in front of him. “Plenty of people have said I look like a <i>“finance guy”</i>, whatever that means.”</div>

<div class="s1 ps">“Huh… I do see it, actually!”, Elijah exclaims, quickly noticing the volume he’s speaking and putting his hand on his mouth. <span style="font-size:.7em">“sorry…”</span></div>

<div class="s1 ps">“Well, I guess it would be less stressful than what I currently do”, Giuseppe added.</div>

<div class="s1 ps">“Oh, I don’t know what your job is, but I assure you it’s better than this”, Elijah joked.</div>

<div class="s1 ps">They shared a genuine laughter; contained as to not wake up the rest of the passengers.</div>

<div class="s1 ps">Giuseppe looks at the window again, the view doesn’t seem to be the same. Could he be just hallucinating? Giuseppe is usually more resistant to sleep deprivation than that. He pulls his sleeve to look at his watch, only to not find it on his wrist.</div>

<div class="s1 ps">“Um… Looking for that?” Elijah points at the watch, gently resting in the seat next to Giuseppe’s snoring friend. “You took it off a few minutes ago.”</div>

<div class="s1 ps">Elijah does the same motion and looks at his own watch. “It’s 3:00 AM. Maybe you need some rest”</div>

<div class="s1 ps">“Perhaps”, Giuseppe said, seemingly remembering something. “But I can’t. Not yet”</div>

<div class="s1 ps">“Can I know why?”. Elijah was curious, leaning forwards in his seat.</div>

<div class="s1 ps">“You actually can’t”. This response startled Elijah. Giuseppe looked away again, staring at the door at the end of the train car. Giuseppe noticed he couldn’t distinguish the faces of other passengers. Their features were there, but he couldn’t make sense of them, as if trying to read the greek or cyrillic script when you’re only familiarized with the latin alphabet; they’re similar enough for you to almost figure out what’s written, but it’s ultimately useless. It felt like having a stroke.</div>

<div class="s1 ps">“I’m sorry if I sounded intrusive!” Elijah carries a concerned expression. He notices Giuseppe staring at the passengers. “A-are you sure you’re okay, sir?”</div>

<div class="s1 ps">“Yes, I just…” Giuseppe seemed to pause for a moment, pondering about whatever it was that was going through his eccentric mind. “Do you know what a cathemeral animal is?”</div>

<div class="s1 ps">The sudden subject shift incited a strong facial response in Elijah. One Giuseppe didn’t quite knew how to interpret. Not that it mattered anyway, as he was not looking at Elijah’s face, but rather at the window behind it, where nothing unusual happened.</div>

<div class="s1 ps">“No… never heard of this word in my life”.</div>

<div class="s1 ps">“Cathemerality is when an animal doesn’t have any set time for sleeping, but rather is awake anywhen he needs to eat, socialize, or any other activity.” Giuseppe explained “Cathemeral animals usually sleep 4 to 6 times a day, taking shorter naps. That’s why you see so many cows awake this late into the night.”</div>

<div class="s1 ps">“That’s all very interesting, Mr. Giuseppe. Are you a naturalist of sorts?” Elijah asked. His voice had, for some reason, a certain nervousness.</div>

<div class="s1 ps">“I do consider myself something along those lines but, if your question is whether or not I work with animals, the answer is no” Giuseppe kept looking at the window, but he didn’t see anything unusual.</div>

<div class="s1 ps">Elijah started to notice Giuseppe rarely ever looked into his eyes. During these last words he was always looking out into the window. “I do love animals, and we have a cat at home, but anything beyond that would be difficult to balance with my work.” Giuseppe kept looking at the window, but he also noticed the rain seemed louder, as if trying to distract his ears from something.</div>

<div class="s1 ps">“You would know a lot about that, right…?” Giuseppe asked “…Having trouble balancing tasks”.</div>

<div class="s1 ps">“W-what do you mean, sir?” Elijah asked, avoiding eye contact. “I-i mean, you barely know me at all! don’t go out mak-”</div>

<div class="s1 ps">“You think I didn’t notice we’re the only ones in this carriage?” Giuseppe side eyes Elijah, staring straight into his eyes, with his face still pointing towards the window. He squints his eyes while his amber irises emit a golden glow. “You can’t make the view convincing and keep the passengers snoring, do you?”.</div>

<div class="s1 ps">Giuseppe stands up; the 188cm man’s head almost touches the ceiling of the relatively short train carriage. He looks at this “Elijah” he has been entertaining and slowly walks towards him, closing the already small gap between their seats.</div>

<div class="s1 ps">“Look, sir, I don’t know what you are talking about…” Elijah says. Giuseppe keeps walking.</div>

<div class="s1 ps">“The carriage never had any other passengers!” He says. Giuseppe keeps walking.</div>

<div class="s1 ps">“Sir, if you don’t back off now I’m gonna have to call the guards” He warns. Giuseppe keeps walking, but he never seems to get any closer. As soon as he noticed this…</div>

<div class="s1 ps"><i>snap</i></div>

<div class="s1 ps">A golden cloud surrounds Giuseppe, shaping itself into the form of an arm, floating above Giuseppe’s shoulder as if connected to his body, holding Elijah’s neck and raising his body off the seat. It’s golden and white and has what seems like a ring made of golden light floating around its wrist.</div>

<div class="s1 ps">Giuseppe takes off his reading glasses as the skin of the man in front of him, being choked by this spectral hand, starts to peel off in small chunks, like dry ink in a wall, revealing underneath a humanoid being with a dark void for a face surrounded by long rotten teeth that forms a spiral going into the void. It was skinny like a starved child, with the outline of its bones visible through its skin. Its nails are long and sharp, like the ones you would see in a movie witch, and it screeched in agony like an animal in a slaughterhouse.</div>

<div class="s1 ps">“It’s been a while since I’ve seen one of <i>these</i>”, Giuseppe said, holding the creature as it struggled to get off his grasp. Reality started to collapse around them, no longer being held together by the demon.</div>

<div class="s1 ps">A dark glow emanated from the creature’s face’s abyss, almost like a shadow. “YOU WILL DIE, EXORCIST” The demon shouted. Its voice echoed as if the unending vacuum in its face acted as a chamber.</div>

<div class="s1 ps">“May the L<span class="sc">ᴏʀᴅ</span> receive me when I do” Giuseppe said, as a cross-shaped knuckleduster appeared in his hand.</div>

<div class="s1 ps">“YOU WILL REGRET <span class="zalgo">T̷H̷I̶S̸ Y̵͎̅O̷͍͠Ú̸ͅ ̴̙͊͗W̵̛͕͋ͅILL R̴̨̡͔̒͑̊E̶̝̳̊́G̸̻̥͚̯͒Ṛ̵͗̊͛̚͝E̷̦̘̞̟̲̅͋́̽̕̚Ț̵̚͜ ̴͍̾̅́̊T̷͉̰̪͉͕̫̹͂H̶̲̑̀͛Ỉ̸̡̩̩̙̣͚̎̾̓͗͝ͅŚ̸̜͓̃ Y̷̢̧̧̢͕̤̖̟͕̣̟̊͊̈́̔͗̏͆Õ̷̪̠͉̠̰̥̤̩̗̘̓̓̃͐̊̚͝Ų̵̢̛̥̳̥͈̣̺͗̉̃̉̓̊̐͋̅̚ ̸̫͉̤̰̬̣̘̩͇̥̀̍͛͗͗̌̓ͅW̵͎͓͎̳̲͖̭̜͔̒͑́͂̍͂́I̵̛̛̩͗͗͝L̴͕̻̠͈̙̥̳̇̎̽̊̀̈́̈́L̷̻̞̯̞̄̇͆͑̿̒̆̇̾͌ ̶̨͉̔̑̽̆R̸͖̦̝̭̫̤̙̪̙̋ͅE̸͓̞̣̤͙̍͛͂̕͠ͅG̵̖̙͍̱̮̣̐̾̓͛̅̒̏̕͜R̵̦̱͇̺̠̖͎̻̣̦̀Ē̶̡̡͚̇́̈͗͘T̸͇̫̫̟̫͇͈̓͐͛̀͂́̉ ̴͉̙̭͍͓̀̄͊͊̔̽̆̊̀͒̑T̵̞͇̫̟̤̯̳̺͉̘̓͗͋̄̀H̵̛̛̱̙͚̜̹̹͓͉͔̫̗̄̐̋͋I̸͉͖̤̩̱̬̝̒S̴̪̹̆̉͒́͘</span>” The monster kept saying;  over and over. Giuseppe clenched his fist and closed his eyes</div>

<div class="s1 ps">Golden light forming a four-pointed star encapsulated his fist as he released a powerful blow,  going through the demon’s chest.</div>

<div class="s1">Giuseppe opened his eyes.</div>

<div class="s1">The sunlight passing through the window of the train carriage made him put his hand in front of his face. He took a look at his watch; it was 9:00 am. There was no one sitting across from him.</div>

<div class="s1">“Oh mio Dio, Giuseppe! Your nose is bleeding” a calm voice said. Giuseppe looked at the direction it came from. A man with a fancy moustache holding a cane. He wore white butler gloves with strange symbols, similar to alchemical circles from the middle ages, patched in gold on the back. “You didn’t even use it this time”</div>

<div class="s1">“I did” said Giuseppe “I was attacked by a <i>Nightmare</i>. Had to use it in a dream”</div>

<div class="s1">“Caspita, Giu! Each day your name as the <i>demon magnet</i> becomes truer!” this man said, already taking a small piece of fabric out of his pocket to clean the nosebleed “Here, let me clean-”</div>

<div class="s1">“Giacomo!” Giuseppe interrupted “You don’t need to worry about me. Plus, all in a night’s work, we’re exorcists. If anything, it helps our job to have swarms of demons coming after us”</div>

<div class="s1">Giuseppe took the cloth and cleaned the nosebleed himself as he stood up and they walked out of the train and into the train station.</div>

<div class="s1">“You know it’s all in a <i><u>day’s</u></i> work, right?” Giacomo said with a smug demeanor and equally smug countenance.</div>

<div class="s1">“I’m aware, but I prefer it the way I said” Giuseppe said, doubling down on his mistake</div>

<div class="s1">“As long as you know you’re wrong, I’m content with it” He said as they left the train station.</div>

Even then, this was their first mission as the newly founded *Wings of Order* – or *Ałe de l’Órdene*, as is written on their sign back in Italy – after they left their previous guild.

Giuseppe stared at the creature with his golden glowing eyes. The room was all of a sudden, as lit as if the lights were on, not only by Giuseppe’s eyes and glowing golden knuckles, but by the enormous wings with feathers made of order carried by the angelic figure that hovered over the exorcist. It was himself, or rather his mind, part of his soul, projected outside of his own body while also maintaining his consciousness. A precise and demanding Mind Doubling technique he had been holding onto until now. Emphasis on <i>demanding</i> as the blood running through his nose started to mix with the blood he was already bathed in.

Knowing that he was on a timer, di Vario did not waste any time, pouring all of his authority in the words that came out of his mouth
